# Event covers: brief and provenance

Four 1920 × 1080 covers (16:9, the event page's shape; the middle 1.9:1 also works for link
previews) for the upcoming events in `server/seed/events.json`. Made 17 Oct 2026.

- **How:** hand-drawn vector artwork (SVG in HTML, rendered with Chromium, saved as JPEG at
  quality 86). The generative tool normally used for assets wasn't connected in that session.
- **Brand:** PANS green `#0e6434`, bright green `#07913c`, navy `#003463`, yellow `#ffbd04`,
  pale green `#e1e8db`, gold `#e0b23c` (dinner only); Poppins 500/600/700; the PANS badge on a
  white circle; "Service to Humanity".
- **Text:** only confirmed facts: the event name and its dates as the site writes them. No
  venues, times or claims.
- **Motifs:** Freshers' Week, a rising sun and confetti (a new start); Pharmalympics, a running
  track and a medal; Health Week, a heart with a pulse line and pharmacy crosses; PANS Dinner,
  an art-deco fan, two glasses and sparkles.
